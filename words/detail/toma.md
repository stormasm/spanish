While both **alimentación** and **toma** can relate to receiving nutrients or power, they are used in very different contexts in Spanish.

Here is a breakdown of how they differ in general usage, medical/infant care, and technical settings:

## **1\. General & Everyday Usage**

* **Alimentación** (*Nutrition / Food intake / Feeding*)  
  * Refers to the overall process or concept of eating, diet, or nutrition.  
  * **Example:** *Una buena alimentación es clave para la salud.* (Good nutrition is key to health.)  
* **Toma** (*Intake / Dose / Sitting*)  
  * Refers to a **single instance, dose, or act** of taking something (food, liquid, medicine).  
  * Derived from the verb *tomar* (to take/drink).  
  * **Example:** *Debes beber agua entre cada toma.* (You should drink water between each intake/dose.)

## **2\. In Infant Care & Medicine**

This is where the two words are most frequently compared:

| Word | Meaning | Context & Example |
| :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Alimentación** | The overall diet or method of feeding | Refers to whether a baby is breastfed, formula-fed, or eating solids. • *Alimentación materna* (Breastfeeding/maternal feeding) • *Alimentación complementaria* (Introducing solid foods) |
| **Toma** | A single feeding session / bottle / nursing bout | Refers to each individual time the baby eats or nurses. • *El bebé hace seis tomas al día.* (The baby feeds six times a day.) • *¿Cuánto biberón toma en cada toma?* (How much milk does he drink in each feeding?) |

**Medical note:** **Toma** is also used for medicine doses (*la próxima toma de la pastilla* \= the next dose of the pill) or taking samples/vitals (*toma de muestra* \= blood draw/sample intake; *toma de presión* \= blood pressure reading).

## **3\. Technical & Engineering Contexts**

Both words are also used in mechanical, electrical, or software engineering, but with distinct meanings:

* **Alimentación** (*Power supply / Feed*)  
  * Refers to the continuous supply of power, fuel, or material to a system.  
  * **Example:** *Fuente de alimentación* (Power supply unit / PSU).  
  * **Example:** *Tubo de alimentación* (Feed tube/pipe).  
* **Toma** (*Outlet / Socket / Intake point / Capture*)  
  * Refers to a specific physical connection point, socket, or intake valve.  
  * **Example:** *Toma de corriente* / *Toma de luz* (Electrical outlet / wall socket).  
  * **Example:** *Toma de agua* (Water intake valve / faucet connection).  
  * **Example:** *Toma de datos* (Data capture / data logging session).

## **Summary Key Difference**

* **Alimentación** \= The **process, diet, or supply system** as a whole (*what* and *how* system/body is nourished).  

* **Toma** \= An **individual session, single dose, or physical intake point** (*one instance* of taking medicine/food, or a physical plug/socket).
