The difference comes down to the reflexive pronoun **me**, which changes the verb from **ir** (to go) to **irse** (to leave/depart).  
In both phrases, **ya** simply adds urgency or immediacy, roughly translating to "right now," "already," or "on it."

### **1\. *Ya voy* ("I am coming" / "I'm on my way")**

* **The verb:** **Ir** (to go)  
* **The perspective:** Unlike English, which uses "to come" (*venir*) when moving toward the listener, Spanish uses **ir** ("to go") anytime you are moving away from your *current* location—even if you are heading straight toward the person calling you.  
* **Literal meaning:** "I go now."  
* **In context:** When someone calls you from another room or texts "Where are you?", saying *"¡Ya voy\!"* means "I am going \[to where you are\] right now" — which translates naturally into English as **"I'm coming\!"**

### **2\. *Ya me voy* ("I am leaving")**

* **The verb:** **Irse** (to leave / to go away)  
* **The perspective:** Adding the reflexive pronoun **me** shifts the focus from your *destination* to your *point of departure*. *Irse* specifically emphasizes the act of exiting or separating yourself from the place you are currently in.  
* **Literal meaning:** "I take myself away now."  
* **In context:** When you are at a party, at work, or hanging out with friends and stand up to head out the door, saying *"Ya me voy"* means **"I'm leaving"** or **"I'm heading out."**

### **Quick Summary**

| Phrase | Verb | Focus | English Equivalent |
| :---- | :---- | :---- | :---- |
| **Ya voy** | *ir* (to go) | Movement toward a destination (often the listener) | "I'm coming\!" / "I'm on my way\!" |
| **Ya me voy** | *irse* (to leave) | Departure from your current location | "I'm leaving." / "I'm heading out." |

*(Note: Because **irse** focuses purely on leaving, you generally don't attach a destination to it. You say "Voy a casa" \[I'm going home\], not "Me voy a casa"—unless you are emphasizing the act of leaving where you currently are to go home, like "Bueno, ya me voy a casa" \[Well, I'm heading off home now\].)*
